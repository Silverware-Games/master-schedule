# Puzzlenox

<img width="384" alt="Puzzlenox" src="https://silverwaregames.com/assets/images/puzzlenox-icon.png" />

**Posture:** Released mobile puzzle game; design exploration continues<br>
**Platform:** iOS / Android

Puzzlenox combines three puzzle systems that gradually imply a larger path and mystery. Its public promise is clean puzzle play without ads or in-app purchases.
