## Posture Vocabulary

- **Actively maintained:** receiving current fixes, polish, content, or operational attention.
- **Private alpha:** playable by a limited invited group while core systems, content, and direction are still being tested.
- **Playable prototype:** functional exploration whose final product shape is still evolving.
- **Concept:** documented creative direction without a public playable build.
- **Released:** publicly shipped; this does not imply development has stopped.
