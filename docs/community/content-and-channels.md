# Content And Channels

Silverware Games shares games, artwork, development thinking, feedback, experiments, and conversations in several formats. This page is the canonical guide to what is published and where to find it.

## Main Destinations

- [Silverware Games](https://silverwaregames.com): studio news, project articles, Coffee Doodle Art archives, and playable-game links.
- [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@MichaelPlzNo): GameDev Feedback, doodle streams, development discussions, experiments, and short-form videos.
- [Coffee Doodle Art](https://coffeedoodle.art): artwork and the evolving public home for the Coffee Doodle Art practice.
- [Coffee Doodle Art roadmap](https://github.com/Silverware-Games/coffeedoodle-art-issue-tracker): public ideas, issues, and improvement work for the art site.
- [GameDev Feedback](https://github.com/Silverware-Games/Gamedev-Feedback): submission queue and public tracker for feedback episodes.

The main website header and footer contain the currently promoted social profiles. Prefer those links over copying a long social-account list into documentation that will become stale.

## Content Lines

### Studio And Project Updates

Articles explain what Silverware Games is building, why a project exists, and what changed. Project-specific destinations live in the [IP Hub](../ip/IP-HUB.md).

### Coffee Doodle Art

Coffee Doodle Art is an ongoing creative practice by Lily and Michael built around frequent, playful drawing rather than perfection or rigid production quotas. Work appears in themed website collections, videos, streams, and the dedicated art site.

### GameDev Feedback

GameDev Feedback offers practical, opinionated feedback for independent games, with emphasis on onboarding, clarity, UX, game feel, and communicating a game's strongest idea. See the dedicated [GameDev Feedback overview](./gamedev-feedback.md).

### Streams, Conversations, And Experiments

YouTube also hosts doodle streams, guest conversations, creative-process discussions, development retrospectives, parody, and one-off experiments. These formats are intentionally flexible.

## Publishing Rhythm

Silverware Games does not promise a fixed posting quota. Content follows active work and creative momentum. A quiet channel may mean effort has shifted to development, client work, or another format.

When publishing:

- Lead with the clearest idea rather than posting everywhere by default.
- Link back to the best canonical home for the project or artwork.
- Cross-post only when the format suits the destination.
- Keep public trackers and permanent project pages more durable than social posts.
- Update this page when a primary destination or content line materially changes.
