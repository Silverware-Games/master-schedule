Gamedev Feedback

Gamedev Feedback is a Silverware Games video series where we play indie games and give honest usability and design feedback while playing.

The goal is simple:

Help developers see their game through the eyes of a new player.

If we get stuck, confused, or lost — that feedback can help improve onboarding, UI, and player understanding.

Philosophy

We aim for respectful criticism and constructive feedback.

No game is perfect.
If your game were perfect, feedback wouldn’t be useful.

Our goal is to help developers make their games clearer, stronger, and easier for players to understand.

The Algorithm Problem

We want these games to be seen.

That means we sometimes use:

exaggerated thumbnails

clickable titles

algorithm-friendly formats

The goal isn't clickbait — it's visibility for indie games.
