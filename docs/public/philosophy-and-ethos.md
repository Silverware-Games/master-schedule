# Philosophy And Ethos

Silverware Games is guided by a simple working philosophy:

- be kind
- be curious
- ship useful and playful things
- protect the creative spark

## Practical Principles

- Fun is a core product requirement.
- Experiments are allowed to be weird.
- Progress can be non-linear and still real.
- Collaboration should be respectful and direct.

## Culture Summary

SWG aims to keep quality high without removing personality from the work.
