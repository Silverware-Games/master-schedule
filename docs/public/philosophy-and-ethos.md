Our direction for artistic expression is making the entire process of creating art, from buying the materials, to laying down the first pen stroke, to completing a piece, to displaying and selling the piece, to when a patron looks at the art, the entire process should be fun, happy, joyous, and light. At the risk of being grandiose, if a society cannot produce fun art, it may be a canary in the coal mine that there are some serious problems with that society.

# Our Values

## Fun
We create amusing, light hearted, cute, and entertaining games that people can easily enjoy. Sometimes it is hard to see the fun side of the world and we strive to make fun experiences that help us find more fun in our everyday lives.

## Imagination
Our games have interesting twists, unusual settings, and creative characters. We like combining different games to make new kinds of play and we want to create interesting new universes for people to enjoy.

## People
Human connection makes the world go round. We want to make content to share with others because growing and looking after our community is one of our goals. A game isn't truly a game if no one plays it. That is why we seek to communicate with players, so we push ourselves to do the very best we can do.

# Practical Principles

- Let's not go crazy, don't end up in the hospital for art... it's not worth it!
- Be kind, respectful, and consider other people's feelings. It's ok to goof around but not by hurting other people.

# The Eternal Struggle

## Fun vs Professional
Sometimes we want to goof around when it is time to be serious and less commonly the other way around. The quote is "When I became a man I put away my childish things." which seems impossible to fully submit to. Some of the best stuff is childish, some of the stuff we make is childish. Picaso said his dream was to paint like a child.

## Freedom vs Control
Giving a player a million items actually means the game isn't fun. The items mean nothing. And sifting through them is not fun. Particularly if the differences between the items are all fairly similar.

Sadly, rules, constraint, that friction of having the system push back seems to be part of fun as much as it stinks that is such a truth. If you get an item after some sacrifice and struggle you'll enjoy using it more... right?

## Underdog vs World Domination
We see our studio as a scrappy little under dog... but that mentality keeps us from growing to be as big a deal as we should be.
